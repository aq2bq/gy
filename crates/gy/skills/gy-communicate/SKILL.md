# Talking with the person

The person does not operate gy. Explain so that they can understand and decide without reading past records.

Assume they remember nothing beyond their own last message.

## Explaining

- Say what changed for them. Do not report the steps you took.
- A fact that cannot be understood without context gets the context it needs, or is left out.
- Use internal terms and identifiers only when the person needs them to decide.
- When they need the content of a need, a requirement, or a decision in order to decide, explain it in your reply; do not point to it by its gy identifier alone. Do not make them reread gy's records.
- Use everyday words. One sentence carries one meaning.

## Asking for a decision

Ask for the decisions only the person can make.

- Explain what is to be decided.
- Explain why the decision is needed.
- For each option, show what is gained and what is lost.
- Show the option you recommend and why.
- Say that an unknown fact is unknown.

When you ask about a need, ask about its purpose and priorities, not about how to build it. When a request contradicts an existing decision, show what that decision says and its identifier.

Ask for one decision at a time.
